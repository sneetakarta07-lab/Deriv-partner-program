What Is the Deriv Partner Program?

The Deriv Partner Program is a free affiliate and introducing broker program run by Deriv — one of the world's most established online trading brokers, founded in 1999. The program allows individuals, content creators, website owners, social media influencers, and business owners to earn commissions by referring new traders to Deriv's platforms.

When someone signs up as a Deriv partner, they receive a unique referral tracking link. Every time a new trader registers and starts trading using that link, the partner earns a percentage of the revenue generated from that trader's activity — for as long as the trader remains active on the platform. This is what makes the Deriv Partner Program particularly attractive: the commissions are lifetime, not just a one-time payment.

Key Fact: The Deriv Partner Program currently has over 117,000 active partners worldwide and paid out commissions totalling millions of dollars across regions including South Asia, Southeast Asia, Africa, and Latin America in 2024–2025.

Deriv offers multiple professional trading platforms including MT5, cTrader, DTrader and DBot — making it easy to attract traders of all levels.

Deriv is regulated in multiple jurisdictions including Malta (MFSA), Labuan (LFSA), and Vanuatu (VFSC), which gives it the credibility and trust factor that makes conversion significantly easier compared to unregulated brokers. Partners do not need to be trading experts — the program is open to anyone who can refer clients through any channel, including websites, blogs, YouTube, social media, or word of mouth.

Master Partner Programme — Build a Sub-Partner Network

Beyond direct referrals, the Deriv Partner Program offers a Master Partner Programme that allows you to recruit other partners (called sub-partners) and earn 20% of their total commission earnings on top of your own direct commissions.

How Master Partner Works

When you recruit a sub-partner and they start earning commissions by referring their own traders, you earn an additional 20% of whatever they earn. This 20% is paid entirely by Deriv — it does not reduce the sub-partner's earnings at all.

For example: if you have 10 active sub-partners each earning $500 per month, you earn an extra $1,000 per month in network commissions without doing anything additional. There is no cap on the number of sub-partners you can recruit.

How to Join the Deriv Partner Program — Step by Step

Joining the Deriv Partner Program is completely free and takes less than 10 minutes. Here is the exact process you need to follow to get your account approved and your referral links ready.

  1. Visit the Partner Hub: Go to Deriv's official partner registration page. If you already have a Deriv trading account, use the same email address to link your partner account to your existing profile.
  2. Fill in Your Details: Provide your name, email address, country, and basic information about how you plan to promote Deriv (website, social media, word-of-mouth, etc.).
  3. Choose Your Commission Plan: Select whether you want Revenue Share, Turnover, or CPA. You can change this later from your dashboard.
  4. Verify Your Identity: Depending on your region, you may need to submit a proof of identity (passport or national ID) and proof of address for compliance purposes.
  5. Access Your Dashboard: Once approved, log into your Partner's Hub to generate tracking links, download banners, view reports, and monitor your referred traders and commissions.
  6. Start Promoting: Copy your referral link and start promoting on your chosen channels. Use the marketing materials provided in the dashboard for the best results.

Important: Deriv does not allow partners to use paid search ads bidding on Deriv-branded keywords (e.g. "Deriv," "Deriv broker"). You must use organic SEO, social media, content marketing, or unbranded paid traffic to promote your referral links.

What is the Deriv Partner Program? +
The Deriv Partner Program is a free affiliate and IB program offered by Deriv, one of the world's largest online trading platforms. Partners earn lifetime commissions (up to 45% revenue share) by referring new traders to Deriv's trading platforms including MT5, cTrader, and DTrader.
How much can I earn as a Deriv partner? +
Earnings vary based on the number and activity level of traders you refer. The Revenue Share plan pays up to 45% of Deriv's monthly net revenue per trader, for life. Top partners in active regions earn $5,000–$50,000+ per month. There is no cap on earnings, and the tiering programme adds up to 18% in additional bonuses as you scale.
Is the Deriv Partner Program free to join? +
Yes, completely free. There are no registration fees, monthly charges, or hidden costs associated with joining the Deriv Partner Program. You sign up, get approved, and immediately access your dashboard and referral links.

Deriv pays CFD commissions (MT5 and cTrader trades) daily. Options commissions (DTrader, DBot) are paid monthly. You can withdraw via bank wire, Skrill, Neteller, Bitcoin, Tether (USDT), and other methods. You can promote Deriv to traders in 190+ countries.
